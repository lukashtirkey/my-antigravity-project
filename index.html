About Don Bosco Skill Mission

The R&D Center of
Don Bosco Tech Society

Don Bosco Skill Mission (DBSM) is the premier research and development center of Don Bosco Tech (DB Tech Society). This is the first master skill training facility set up by DB Tech, a non-profit organization with its head office in New Delhi and working across India in the area of skill development.

DBSM is a skill training center with a difference, established with the explicit aim of promoting learning, research, and innovation in the skill development sector.

🎯 Our Institutional Objectives

01

Research & Innovation

Promote research, training, innovation, and professional development across technical and vocational domain areas.

02

Capacity Development of Trainers

Capacity development of trainers in technical & domain knowledge, facilitation, and hand-holding trainees for placement & post-placement support.

03

Conferences & Exhibitions

Organize and promote conferences, seminars, lectures, public debates, and skill exhibitions to foster industry discourse.

04

National & Global Collaboration

Cooperate and collaborate with other leading national and/or international institutions for vocational excellence.

05

Assessment & Staff Training

Conducting comprehensive competency assessment and specialized training for institutional staff and management.

Common Enquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our admission criteria, scholarship funding, residential facilities, and job placements.

Yes, absolutely. Qualified candidates under our scholarship criteria receive 100% free tuition, course study materials, residential hostel accommodation, three daily meals in our dining hall, and official government-recognized certifications without paying any fees.

DBSM offers inclusive skill pathways. Depending on the trade, eligibility ranges from Below 8th Standard to 10th (SSLC), 12th (PUC), ITI, or Graduation. Our admissions counselor will help match your current education with the best course.

We maintain an active placement team that collaborates with over 500 corporate hiring partners. Every student who completes their course and soft-skills assessments receives placement assistance until they are successfully employed in domestic or international positions.

Our campus is located at Kothanuru, Bannerghatta Road, Bengaluru — 560076. Outstation trainees from across Karnataka and India can register online through our form or call our counselor at +91-9894442603. Free hostel accommodation is provided on campus.

Graduates receive government-recognized certificates issued by Don Bosco Tech Society, accredited by the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) and relevant Sector Skill Councils (SSC), which are valid nationwide and recognized internationally.
